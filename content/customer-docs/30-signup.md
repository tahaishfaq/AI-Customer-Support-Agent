# Sign up and log in

![Sample: agents dashboard after you sign in](/customer-docs/agents-list.svg)

## Create an account

1. Open Aide and go to **Register**
2. Enter your email and password (or use Google if enabled on your deployment)
3. Verify your email if prompted
4. You land in your workspace dashboard

## Log in

Use **Login** with the same email and password (or Google).

## Workspaces

Your account belongs to a **workspace**. Agents, knowledge, credentials, and billing live under that workspace. Invite policies depend on your plan and admin settings.

## Tips

- Use a real email you can access for password reset
- Keep one primary workspace for production agents

# Inbox and human handoff

Aide can escalate chats to a human when the visitor asks for a person or when tools / policy require it.

![Sample: Inbox waiting list and human reply](/customer-docs/inbox.svg)

## What visitors experience

- A clear message that a human will help (or how to continue)
- The conversation appears in your **inbox** / desk views for operators

## Owner tips

- Keep AI knowledge strong so handoffs stay for edge cases
- Monitor the inbox regularly during launch
- Do not promise live humans if no one is staffing the desk

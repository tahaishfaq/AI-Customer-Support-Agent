# Analytics and billing

## Analytics

Aide provides conversation and agent analytics in the dashboard so you can see volume and outcomes at a glance. Exact charts depend on your deployment.

## Billing

Plans and checkout are managed under **Settings → Billing** (or the billing pages shown in your workspace). Keep payment details up to date so agents stay available on paid plans.

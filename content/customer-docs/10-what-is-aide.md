# What is Aide?

Aide is a platform to create **AI customer support agents** for your business. Visitors chat with your agent on your site or in the Aide studio. The agent answers from knowledge you provide, can call connected tools (when you enable them), and can escalate to a human when needed.

![Sample flow: create agent → knowledge → test → embed](/customer-docs/docs-home.svg)

![Sample: agents list with New agent button](/customer-docs/agents-list.svg)

## What you can do

- Create one or more **agents** with their own personality and knowledge
- Upload documents or **crawl a public website** into knowledge
- Customize and **embed** a webchat widget
- Connect **tools** (HTTP APIs, GitHub MCP, and more)
- Review conversations in the **inbox** and see basic **analytics**

## Who Aide is for

Teams that want AI support grounded in *their* content — not generic web answers — with clear confirmation before risky actions.
